## 01 / The Concept

**A digital space for people who love getting lost in a good book.**

BookStore is a web development project built around the experience of exploring books through a modern digital interface. The goal is to bring together thoughtful design, intuitive navigation, and an enjoyable browsing experience.

> *Less clutter. More stories. Endless possibilities.*
